Overview

Credit Key is a B2B payment solution that enables merchants to offer fast, transparent, and flexible payment terms to business buyers without taking on credit risk.

With Credit Key integrated into Odoo, your sales reps can offer Net 30 and extended payment terms directly within Odoo — no switching systems, no manual credit checks, and no offline approvals.

The result is higher conversion rates, larger average order values, and faster purchasing decisions.

Get guaranteed payment while Credit Key handles underwriting and risk.

Configure and deploy quickly with native Odoo payment workflows.

Payment Method Configuration

Configure Credit Key directly from Odoo Accounting settings.

  1. Navigate to Accounting → Configuration
  2. Open the Payment Acquirers menu
  3. Configure the Credit Key payment provider
  4. Add your public and private API keys
  5. Select the applicable pricelist configuration

Credit Key Checkout Experience

Customers can select Credit Key during checkout and complete the payment flow directly through the Credit Key platform.

Flexible Financing Options

Buyers can choose from multiple payment plans including installment options and extended financing terms.

On-Site Messaging

Display financing messaging directly on product pages, cart pages, and checkout to increase average order value and encourage conversions.
