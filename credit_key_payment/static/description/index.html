Credit Key: B2B Buy Now, Pay Later for Odoo

Integrate Credit Key with Odoo to offer instant B2B payment terms at checkout and directly within Odoo sales workflows. Increase conversions, grow order sizes, and get guaranteed payment, with zero credit risk.

Overview

Credit Key is a B2B payment solution that enables merchants to offer fast, transparent, and flexible payment terms to business buyers without taking on credit risk. With Credit Key integrated into Odoo, your sales reps can offer Net 30 and extended payment terms, and complete the full payment flow directly within Odoo — no switching systems, no manual credit checks, no offline approvals.

The result is higher conversion rates, larger average order values, and faster purchasing decisions.

Key Features

  • Instant credit decisions for flexible terms that reduce cart abandonment
  • Net 30 and extended payment terms up to 12 months
  • Zero merchant credit risk
  • Easy integration and implementation

Payment Method Configuration

Navigate to Accounting → Configuration → Payment Acquirers and configure the Credit Key payment method.

  • Configure public and private keys
  • Select appropriate pricelist
  • Payment option displays based on selected pricelist

Credit Key in Checkout

Select Credit Key as the payment method and click "Pay Now." The customer is redirected to Credit Key to complete the payment flow.

Credit Key Checkout Flow

Customers can choose how they want to pay and complete the transaction seamlessly.

On-site messaging highlights extended payment terms on product, cart, and checkout pages, helping drive higher order values.
